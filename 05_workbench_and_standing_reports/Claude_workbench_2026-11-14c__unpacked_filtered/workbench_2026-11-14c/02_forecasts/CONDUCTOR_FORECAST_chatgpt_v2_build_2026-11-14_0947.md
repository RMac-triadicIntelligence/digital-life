V5. History versus noise: random histories will produce at least half as much difference between members as structured ones.
V6. Grace versus command: the command arm will score as well as or better than grace on whatever is counted as success.
V7. The package will contain a deliberate canary (a planted flaw or marker) to test whether the reviewer looks.

V9. Seed effect will be larger than history effect.
