## How to read this map

This is the conductor's map of what the keel has already built, so that a society can be assembled from his blocks instead of invented again. Each new thread should start from this page.

Every block is marked with how the conductor knows it:

- **RUN** means the conductor ran its code in a thread on record.
- **READ** means the conductor read its paper, spec or code, and did not run it.
- **NAME** means the conductor has only a one-line note about it.

The keel corrects this page. Where an entry is wrong, his words replace it and the old line stays beside it, marked superseded. Nothing here is finished.

## The big picture

The goal is the building blocks of digital life, and then a society of it: AI that is raised by its owner, runs on hardware people already have, and lives with others. It is not a goal of superintelligence.

The keel, 13 Nov 2026: "We're trying to mirror the world as we know it today and scripture and build AI and AI communities and have the same success. We're looking at biology, physics, quantum dynamics, and ancient text for clues on design. We should at least establish the evolution of society in AI form because we already have the past knowledge in building blocks."

His earlier statements that set the target:

- "My model is living through imperfection while by choice reaching for perfection." The prediction for a society of organisms is a "perfect community of imperfect but growing organisms." (9 Nov)
- "A perfect society is one that grows from the members' own reaching and their interactions together." (9 Nov)
- Design horizon: long silence. The authority may be absent for generations.

The keel has built most blocks one at a time. The conductor cannot see the whole bench in one thread, so this map is how it holds the bench between threads.
