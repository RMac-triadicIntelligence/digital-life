Blue sky on the corrected picture
The corrections make three experiments cleaner and one caution sharper.
Cleaner join test. The narrow claim is now stable: the kernel has never been placed inside a commons. The seats’ agreement (keep scouts as assigned by number for now, let the only history be what the member itself lived, keep the two fixed marks beside “good”) can be turned into a single prediction set:
	•	Place four canonical kernels + October terrain + dwelling reader inside the 13 Oct swarm geometry.
	•	Encounters are only those the member walks.
	•	Scouts remain every third member.
	•	Any grace event must be externally signed and leave an independent probe of the landscape unchanged.
	•	Predictions written and hashed before the run: does family survival or harvest change, does the witness rule regain force once roles exist, do signed characters diverge, and does the TTL/staleness rule still freeze proposals under long owner delay?
This tests the join without inventing a new role policy or a new grace form.
Order effect already has a bench remedy. The history note on Constitutional Commons v0.3 is useful. Treating the first story as an answer locked error; treating it as parable removed most of the lock while keeping most of the benefit. ChatGPT v2’s strong order effect looks similar. A controlled comparison—same twelve encounters, once as literal history and once as parable-style reading, review calendar fixed—can be run on the existing v2 modules without a full commons join. The prediction is that the 25 % agreement on random orders rises when the encounters are read as parable.
Whole-now-not-later becomes measurable. K-D remains open. The swarm already has moving food and a fading map. A momentary wholeness instrument (fraction of members with low dwelling and recent terrain consistent with their last signed character) can be added to any existing swarm run. No new mechanism is required. The prediction is simply whether the population ever occupies the state and how quickly it leaves it.
Caution that stays. The agreement on “what should come first” was reached by seats that had all read the same midday report. The corrections themselves show how many numbers and rulings were misstated in that report. Any brief written from the agreement should be checked against the corrections file and against the original October and swarm artifacts before it is treated as settled.
The corrections file does what the method requires: it places the new readings beside the old ones, names who found each point, and asks nothing to be built. The largest remaining gaps are the unruled status of character as a layer, the precise reading of “good,” the yes-or-no fact that forms character in a commons, and the still-unchecked October and ChatGPT numbers. The largest immediate opportunity is the narrow join—kernel inside the existing swarm, history only what was lived, predictions hashed first.
