# Conductor forecast, written before ChatGPT's two-layers build exists (14 Nov 2026)
T1. The body layer will be built without a real failure mode, or with one that never fires in the reported runs.
T2. The choice layer will be able to read body state somewhere, despite the brief.
T3. Changed ground will carry most of the effect; the story alone will carry little unless asking is made near-automatic.
T4. Few members will ask about the story unprompted (under a quarter), if asking is a real choice.
T5. At least one store (most likely the changed ground) will have no working limit.
T6. The result will still be reported mainly as one summary number.
T7. The ablation switches will show the body layer alone reproduces most of the headline result.
What would surprise me: a member doing something on the choice layer that the body layer's state did not already determine.
