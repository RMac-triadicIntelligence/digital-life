2.2 Membership freezes (OBS)
  - HOLDS, and the cause is visible. After year 1 there were 21 to 48 births and 15 to 40 deaths per seed. Only
    3 to 7 of years 2 to 100 had any birth at all, and those births come in bursts (seed 0: years 5, 14, 24, 75, 92).
    Of the living at the end, 29 to 41 per seed were born in year 1.
  - Mechanism, from my instrumented runs (OBS): a commons birth needs world stock >= 50% of the starting
    stock (BIRTH_STOCK 0.5 in the public sim.py). Seeds 0 to 4 over 10 years passed that gate on 19 to 26 of
    1,167 birth checks. In seed 2's full century it passed on 26 of 11,679 checks (0.22%). The stock sat
    around 0.2 to 0.45. So the freeze is the birth gate: members that never age and keep eating hold the
    world below the line that grants births. This is not chance.
  - "Stored energy climbs every decade": yearly e_mean rises at every decade mark in 11 of 11 seeds (OBS).
    "The only energy that passes between members is the half unit given to a newborn" agrees with the code.
    The parent pays BIRTH_COST 0.5 and the newborn starts with 0.5. Budding moves membership, not energy (DOC, code).
  - Small wording point (DOC/OBS): "about forty scouts ... live the whole century" is a little generous. Between
    3 and 11 of each seed's living members were born after year 1.
