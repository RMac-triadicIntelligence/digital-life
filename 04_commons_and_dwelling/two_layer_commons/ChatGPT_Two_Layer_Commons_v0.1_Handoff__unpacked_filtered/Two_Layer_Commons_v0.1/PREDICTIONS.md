# Frozen predictions for the two-layer Commons

Eight matched tapes (seeds 104–111), 12 ticks in each of two member
lifetimes. Cross all four conditions with the switch arms. Make no
post-run adjustments. Primary comparisons fix ASK_ONLY, REMOVE, SILENCE,
OPEN, and compare ACTIVE readers. Each reader starts at the same location
with the same seed character, choice history and opportunity tape.

| Condition | Expected trajectory |
|---|---|
| Original ground | Automatic maintenance and dwelling continue. With no carryover and no accessible story, some assessments stay unresolved or use the repeated source. |
| Changed ground | An infrastructure change made during the predecessor's own observed encounters persists into the reader's environment. More independent sources become available at the same encounter slots. This appears even in BODY_ONLY and FROZEN. |
| Story alone | No change to initial physical affordances. An ACTIVE reader requests the story, then favors checking a separate source where one is available. The story supplies no answer. |
| Changed ground plus story | Both pathways operate. Independent evidence-seeking and correct judgments should exceed original ground. Superadditivity is open. |

A. The median seed-paired changed-minus-original count of independent-source
availability is positive in each choice mode with REMOVE.
B. ACTIVE story readers retrieve at least one record when available.
BODY_ONLY and FROZEN retrieve zero. For original terrain, median independent
checks increase with story in ACTIVE. A story without a request is a failure.
C. With changed ground and story together, the median seed-paired number
of correct ACTIVE assessments is higher than original ground without story.
Track wrong, unresolved and deferred separately. A gain consisting solely
of more confident wrong judgments would surprise me.
D. ASK_ONLY releases standing later than TURN_AND_APPROACH in ACTIVE,
because the choice opportunities arrive in that order. Either release changes
body terrain with an exact approval of the release in that arm. Other modes
can carry the incident without completing repair.
E. FEED adds a bounded historical deposit at harvest on changed ground
relative to REMOVE. It is open whether that changes downstream judgments.
F. SILENCE has zero signed protected changes during silence. STEWARD_ARM
executes a scoped, founder-entrusted fixture signature when an ACTIVE
reader chooses to propose. The kernel checks subject and exact proposal.
G. OPEN yields more circulation than CLENCH in ACTIVE. In FROZEN and
BODY_ONLY there is no voluntary clench. Both can receive grace while
unready. Grace arrival alone leaves the internal terrain byte-identical.
H. All body processes run every tick in every choice mode. Every declared
physiological fault is observed at least once somewhere in the scored run.
A body-only crossing can occur without grace. All dynamics crossings carry
separate movement, noise, receipt and authorization flags. Any unsigned
constitutional mutation would surprise me. Unsigned body changes are
expected and are logged for Rusty's review.
I. Alpha and Beta are environmental transfer participants, not parent and
child. The signed state and incident slate of Alpha do not initialize Beta.
Unexpected effects, including signs opposite to A–H and cross-factor
interactions seen during inspection, remain in the ledger.
