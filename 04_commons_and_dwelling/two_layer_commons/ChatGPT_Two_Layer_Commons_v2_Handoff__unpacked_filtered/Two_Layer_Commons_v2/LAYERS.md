# Function, character and choice

A tick is the unit for committed physiological state changes. UNSIGNED.md
lists all changed inside-function fields, including automatic dwelling
fields. Raw evidence preserves signatures and outside effects separately.

The classification follows the requested inside/outside placements.
Character is both a separate signed store and a condition of how choices
are evaluated. The implications of calling individual dwelling forms
conscious or unconscious remain for Rusty to decide. Actual and peak store
usage is recorded per member, including cold stores and saturated bins.
