# Layers, locations and failure paths

The four placements from the brief are used. The scheduler, test owner and
observer operate outside the member. The observer sees all four placements,
including hidden tape truth for scoring. Members receive inputs through the
ports in INTERFACE.md. The canonical kernel spans internal proposals and
constitutional state, plus externally signed decisions and witness records.

| Layer and place | Store or process | Capacity or bound | Failure or refusal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body inside | Energy reserve and intake | 0–8 units | Contact can fail to deliver intake. |
| Body inside | Homeostatic regulation | One invocation per tick | Fault raises expenditure and interrupts evidence uptake and adaptation on that tick. |
| Body inside | Own terrain adaptation and burden | 0–6 each | Adaptation may stall under regulation fault and saturates. Abrasion adds burden. A checked release can clear it. |
| Body inside | Evidence reserve and regime | 0–8 units and one Boolean | Regulation fault suppresses evidence update, which can stall or prevent entry into the settled regime. |
| Body inside | Hand and circulation | 0–2 retained units | Circulation fault prevents uptake and forwarding on that tick. In the CLENCH arm, yesterday's gesture can physically obstruct today's forwarding. |
| Body outside | Shared ground channel and harvested deposit | 0–8 and 0–3 units | Ground process may miss a scheduled modification or harvest through the tape's ground fault. Wear saturates. |
| Body outside | Grace in the Commons | 0–6 units carried between ticks | Saturation discards spill above capacity and the trace counts it. Carrier blockage returns rejected provision to the Commons port. |
| Body outside | Witnessed abrasion | One archived encounter per member lifetime and one active incident | Second simultaneous incident would exceed protocol capacity. Only one is introduced. The historical witness can remain after current standing is restored. |
| Choice outside | Kept story and answer-free projection | One immutable story per world | Retrieval can find no story. Publication requires two observed contact opportunities. |

| Outside observer | Test tape, trace and result collection | 12 ticks per member, one Alpha plus 192 Beta variants per seed, declared seed list | Run aborts on invariant failure. Output receives a file digest once complete. |

A body regime crossing is a change of an operational settled Boolean.
The trace records threshold movement across sampled ticks. This build
does not identify a mathematical separatrix. The brief contains both the
unaided-crossing ruling and the signature-for-separatrix wording. No
mathematical crossing is claimed, and a protected kernel commitment
always takes the external signature and witnessed checkpoint path.
