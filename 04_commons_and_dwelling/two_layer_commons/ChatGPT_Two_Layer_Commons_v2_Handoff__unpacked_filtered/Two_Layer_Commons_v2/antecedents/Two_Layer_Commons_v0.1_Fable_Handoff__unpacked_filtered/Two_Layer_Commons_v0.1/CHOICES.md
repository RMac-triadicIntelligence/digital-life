# Member choices

The trace lists subject, option set and selection for each action.

| Choice | Options |
|---|---|
| Request the story on the first scheduled opportunity | Ask or pass. The active policy asks at the public marker in every world, including worlds where retrieval finds no story. |
| Seek evidence | Wait, check the repeated source, and check a separate source when access is available. |
| Respond to a selected reading | Hold, judge zero, or judge one. The active reader that attended to the story holds on repeat-source evidence. |
| Respond to abrasion | Turn and approach or leave, followed when still needed by ask for release or leave. |
| Hand action in CLENCH | Open or clench. The choice becomes next tick's physical obstruction. |
| Request a protected proposal on the scheduled opportunity | Propose or defer, based on settled sensation. |

ACTIVE records explicit decisions. BODY_ONLY emits none. FROZEN applies
the fixed repeated-source policy and the source's fixed answer mapping,
with those records marked `frozen_default`. It has no story, hand, repair
or proposal decisions. Both comparison modes retain all physiological work.
Source selection changes which observations are acquired, and correctness is
scored against tape truth rather than attached to a bodily state.
