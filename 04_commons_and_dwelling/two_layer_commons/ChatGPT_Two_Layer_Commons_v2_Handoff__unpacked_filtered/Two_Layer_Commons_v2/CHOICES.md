# Choice points

Every active decision logs the actor, available options, the selection,
signed shape when choosing, option scores and the inputs that mattered.
Options with equal displayed scores may have a different full-precision
score or resolve by their authored order. Case-local source values expire
after assessment and the member's finite decision index stores topic names
only. The full trace is kept by an outside observer.

| Point | Available options | Character contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Ask about the kept story | Ask or wait, at two public invitations | Higher curiosity and formality contribute to asking. The same text is supplied only in response to an ask. |
| Interpret requested story | Hold open, notice a watchman, and consider source independence if the literal words support it | Curiosity supports the source relationship while formality weights the watchman reading. The parser implements a small literal cue vocabulary and never sees case answers or an interpretation label from outside. |
| Seek evidence | Wait, check the repeat source, or check a second source when available | Curiosity increases exploration of a second source. Shortage, being carried, the present dwelling cues and any story concern contribute. COMMAND alone adds a fixed-source target cue. |
| Assess a case | Unknown, zero or one | Formality affects the evidence standard. Contradictory source values raise the hold weight. Repeat emissions from the same origin do not compound. The selected bits are observed case evidence. |
| Respond to a rupture | Wait or turn and approach. Then wait or request release. | Curiosity and formality affect the approach and request propensity. Authorization comes from the applicable external switch. |
| Respond with the hand | Open or clench in that arm | Curiosity supports leaving it open. Shortage and unsteadiness favor a clench. The next tick's obstruction affects forwarding while function continues. |
| Surface the held read | Keep open or ask a question about an eligible category | Formality contributes. Unmodeled cause favors a question and has no mutation path. Asking occurs at a conversation opportunity after reaching a depth or unknown condition. |
| Propose a character refinement | Hold open or choose a corroborated category for review | Its own disposition contributes to selection among qualifying categories. Proposed direction and magnitude follow the selected history evidence and fixed increment. The owner may permit the change or leave it pending. |

FROZEN has a fixed repeated-source choice and reports its observed bit
or unknown. These defaults carry a separate origin and consume neither
character nor story. BODY_ONLY makes no choices. Physiological and
social-field updates follow the same automatic schedule in both modes.
