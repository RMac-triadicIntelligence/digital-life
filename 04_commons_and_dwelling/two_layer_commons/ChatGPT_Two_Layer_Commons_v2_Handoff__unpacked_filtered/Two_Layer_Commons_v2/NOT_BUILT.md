# What remains outside this experiment

- This is a finite fixture with authored social likelihoods, task
  evidence, character increments and a literal parser. It establishes
  replayable behavior of the code. It does not establish life,
  consciousness, general language understanding, emergent values or a
  universal advantage from training history.
- Two exposures of the same story use immutable text and changing
  signed shape inside one continuing lifetime. The feature extractor
  contains a small vocabulary for this story. A later shift is
  compositional policy behavior under fixed cues. Generalization to a
  new story or unseen semantics is open.
- No biological genealogy, morphology, physical implementation,
  multi-generational succession or indefinite operation. The founder is
  gone before the reader starts. The signed reader profile initializes
  the dispositions independently of the founder. A distinct seed uses two owner-attested birth
  offsets as its matched profile control.
- Steward signing and the tares-after-harvest switch are optional in
  v2 and are left in runnable v0.1. V2 uses silence with no signing as
  laid out in the brief. It preserves observed abrasion outside the
  member after repair; it does not run a harvest experiment.
- No reconstruction of the old 512-dimensional Prayer runtime, full
  TQNM engine, automatic character commits from the older basin paper,
  or directed grace in the real provision arm. The equations and
  mechanisms cited in the brief have local adapters and controls.
- No mathematical separatrix claim. The model labels sampled regime
  transitions separately from exact signed commitments. One operational
  threshold event can occur during independent licensed repair. The
  supplemental zero-provision, body-only condition removes that path.
- Signed character runs through untouched canonical v0.7 method bodies
  with a deterministic test adapter replacing entropy, clock and
  external Ed25519 dependency. The character mechanism issues its
  candidate using the kernel's exact proposal API underneath the
  keyword path. The fixture serializes experimental keys internally as
  hex and uses a local witness. This is an experiment with public test
  keys and trusted-process code, not a deployment security claim.
- The supplemental world-mismatch probe forces a world direction and
  tests the rule with a single observation. The main healthy condition
  never activated world dwelling behavior. The main latency contrast
  changes several dwelling mechanisms at once.
- Provision continues to circulate through the outside Commons. The
  founder-to-reader transfer includes ground and the story only. This
  lifecycle does not measure network propagation through many live
  recipients, a full grace constitution or long-run conservation.
- The historical version and all originally scored arms remain
  runnable. No scored threshold, policy, or schedule was retuned after
  viewing outcomes.

- The local ASK_ONLY and APPROACH models are parallel experimental
  triggers. This experiment treats body-threshold movement and the
  protected separation at the signature seam as distinct observables.
