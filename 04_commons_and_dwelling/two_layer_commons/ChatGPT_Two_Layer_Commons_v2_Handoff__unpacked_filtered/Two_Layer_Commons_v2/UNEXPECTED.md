## Supplemental predictions

- The world recurrence behaved as expected for mismatch, alignment, and
  signed decay in an antiparallel case. Active and Boolean-yoked judgments
  agreed every time. I kept the world-specific efficacy prediction as a
  miss. The hold bit already accounted for the observed response.
- The marginal-story probe shifted early decisions on seven of eight
  tapes. After the second visit, evidence actions remained identical.
  I retained the prespecified late-window miss alongside the early
  difference and the small raw assessment differences.
- The zero-provision body-only probe yielded 12 transitions with no
  signed repair or character effect, as predicted.
