# Frozen component probes

These probes leave the scored design, thresholds, source and evidence
unchanged. The initial results showed world-field variation without
reaching the active threshold for a healthy BOTH reader, and a shift in
story interpretation with no change in task behavior in the primary
ground-matched comparisons. These probes ask whether both distinctions
are reachable under separate, declared conditions.

| Probe | Expected outcome and falsifier |
|---|---|
| Mismatched world | Use the implemented Prayer recurrence with the fixed normalized direction (3,4,0) and an orthogonal world input (-4,3,0) for ticks 0–7. The world field should pass its existing 0.16 active threshold. Thereafter an aligned input should reduce the field. An antiparallel input has the same absolute coherence, but signed decay should increase a positive field. Treat that sign asymmetry as a legacy-mechanism observation. |
| Matched latency of the world | Compare the evolved situation's choice cues with its Boolean-only yoke at the same review times. Present the same single repeat-source observation, an unchanged default signed shape, and no facet hold. Expect at least one judgment to differ during orthogonal mismatch. If both behave the same throughout the window, the world-specific cue is behaviorally redundant in this probe. |
| Marginal story | Reuse the ALT_SEED_LISTEN reader and inherited ground. Compare the complete story with a cue-redacted story and no story. Reuse the exact tapes and training histories of the primary seeds. By the second visit, expect the two requested versions to diverge in interpretation and at least one matched evidence choice, with initial ground, signed character, and body exposure equated. No correct-answer advantage is predicted. An identical action path would retain the main-run null. |
| No provision and no choice | Repeat BOTH as a body-only reader with zero minting and no living or queued predecessor provision. Expect at least one operational transition, zero repair, zero protected approval, and zero reception. A repair-associated or signed transition would fail the unaided check. |
