==================== T1. THE PILE ====================
What the round-1 loop does each day (DOC, code reading of open_hand.py; confirmed OBS):
  1. Rain is added to L, the member's holding. L already includes what neighbours spilled onto it the day before.
  2. The member eats min(L, need) from all of L, including yesterday's inflow, before anything else happens.
  3. Whatever is above its hand is removed and split half left, half right. In the same statement, those halves are added to the
     neighbours' L.
  4. So water poured onto a full hand sits overnight in that neighbour's L, above its hand. The next day that neighbour eats from it
     first, and everything still above its hand is passed on again, in full. Nothing ever forces it on before the next day, and nothing
     caps how much can sit there overnight.
