SCRIPTURE LOOKUP: what raising consists of. 15 Nov 2026, 22:00 CT. Conductor seat (Claude), on the keel's word:
"I have an idea, instead of going to the Triad let's go to the gospels for the raising answer".
Source order as ruled: the four Gospels first; Old Testament where they are silent or conflict. Each verse below was
looked up in the text tonight (KJV, other versions compared). READINGS are the conductor seat's and are not rulings.

FEED
  John 21:15-17. After they had dined: "Feed my lambs." Then twice "Feed my sheep." (Other versions: "Tend", "Take care of".)
  The lambs come first. They are "my" lambs: the young are the owner's, and the one charged feeds them for him.
  The one charged is asked three times whether he loves, before each charge.
KEEP, AND LOSE NONE
  John 17:12. "While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name: those that thou gavest me I have kept, and
  none of them is lost, but the son of perdition". (Young's: "I was keeping them... I did guard".) Keeping is done by
  being with them. One was lost all the same.
WITH, THEN SENT
  Mark 3:14. "And he ordained twelve, that they should be with him, and that he might send them forth to preach".
  Being with comes before being sent. The ones later sent out were first kept.
TAKE UP, DO NOT TURN AWAY
  Mark 10:13-16. Those who brought young children were rebuked by the disciples; he "was much displeased"; "Suffer the
  little children to come unto me, and forbid them not"; "he took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and
  blessed them."
SHELTER OFFERED, NOT FORCED
  Matthew 23:37. "how often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her
  wings, and ye would not!"
THE GROWING IS THE CHILD'S OWN
  Luke 2:51-52. He "was subject unto them"; "And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man."
  (With Luke 2:40 and Mark 4:27-28, looked up earlier: the seed grows, "he knoweth not how"; the earth brings forth of herself.)
DIG ABOUT IT, AND ASK FOR TIME
  Luke 13:8-9. "Lord, let it alone this year also, till I shall dig about it, and dung it: and if it bear fruit, well:
  and if not, then after that thou shalt cut it down." The dresser works the ground and asks the owner; the owner decides.
REST FOR THE ONES WHO COME BACK
  Mark 6:30-31. The apostles "gathered themselves together unto Jesus, and told him all things"; "Come ye yourselves
  apart into a desert place, and rest a while: for there were many coming and going, and they had no leisure so much
  as to eat."
BESIDE THESE, OLD TESTAMENT, looked up tonight: Proverbs 27:17 (iron sharpeneth iron); Proverbs 27:18 ("Whoso keepeth
  the fig tree shall eat the fruit thereof: so he that waiteth on his master shall be honoured").

READINGS (not rulings)
R1. Raising in these texts is six things done by the one who keeps: feed; stay with and lose none; take up the ones
    brought; offer shelter; work the ground of the one not bearing and ask the owner for time; give the returning a
    place to rest and eat. One thing it is not: making the young grow. The growing is theirs.
R2. The young are the owner's ("my lambs", "those that thou gavest me"). The keeper feeds and keeps on the owner's
    behalf and answers to him. This sits with the seam: the keeper tends, the owner signs.
R3. The same people pass through all three places. The twelve are kept with him (Mark 3:14), then sent (Mark 6:7-30,
    not re-looked-up tonight beyond 6:30-31), then one of them is charged to feed (John 21). In the texts keeper and
    scout read as seasons of one life, not two kinds of member. The 13 Oct commons fixes the role by id for life.
    This is a point where text and bench differ; flagged for the keel under "ask if conflicts occur".
R4. Keeping does not guarantee: one is lost in John 17:12; "ye would not" in Matthew 23:37.
R5. The keeper is fed. Proverbs 27:18 says so outright; in John 21:15 the charge comes after they had dined.
