WHAT IS RUN
The 13 Oct commons (V2_FULL) is the baseline and is not edited. commons_raised.py is the diagnostic runner with four
switches added by asserted text swaps (make_raised.py). All four off must reproduce the hash on record.
  H  A HAND HAS A SIZE. After everyone has eaten, whatever a member holds above one hand goes back on the ground
     where it stands, and is taken off its household's harvest count.
  S  THE SENT FEED THE KEEPERS. Before that, a scout's overflow goes to keepers of its own household who hold less
     than a hand, the lowest first, each filled no higher than a hand.
  F  KEEPERS FEED THE LAMBS. An adult keeper holding more than the birth portion gives to the lowest young member of
     its household standing on its cell or the next one, if that young one is under the birth portion: enough to
     bring it up to the birth portion, and never taking the keeper under it.
  W  WITH, THEN SENT. A young member does not range. It goes to the nearest adult keeper of its household and stays
     beside it. When its first season ends it takes up its role. If its household has no adult keeper it lives as October's did.
Everything else is October's: roles by member number, births, the birth rules, food, noise, witness, spacing,
