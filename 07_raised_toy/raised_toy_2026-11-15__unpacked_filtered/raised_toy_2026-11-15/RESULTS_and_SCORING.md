UNEXPECTED (none of these was predicted as it came out)
U1. The hand alone ends the freeze. With no other change, births go from tens to thousands and the stock rises
    to the birth line. It also makes every member mortal: nobody can outlast 40 ticks without food.
U2. The families are lost. Under every step that has a hand, three of the four founding lineages die out within ten
    years in most seeds. It comes with the hand, not with the raising rules. In October's commons all four lineages
    last only because their founders never die.
U3. Keepers live when the sent feed them, and only then. The hand alone leaves keepers dying as before (0.4% reach
    30 days). With the scouts' overflow, 35% do, and keepers are present in every year. A keeper's own harvest is
    unchanged and still under cost (0.018 a tick): it lives on what it is given.
U4. Keeping the young beside a keeper hurt the young. Newborn survival to 30 days, both roles together, falls from
    48% to 31% when that rule is added; for young scouts from 75% to 47%.
U5. Keepers feeding the young hardly happens unless the young are kept beside them (93 units in three seeds), and
    when they are, it is small (about a tenth of what scouts give keepers) and does not make up for U4.

  holds up here: the keeper who is given something to eat stops dying at once.
- NOT SHOWN: that raising, as built here, is what keeps anyone alive. The two raising rules did nothing (F) or harm (W).
- The definitions are the conductor seat's candidates and several look wrong on their face after the run, which is
  not the same as knowing better ones. A hand of 1.0 leaves every member 40 ticks from death (hole T1). The young
  are kept on a keeper's ground, and a keeper's ground is the poorest in the commons: nothing in the toy gives a
  keeper a place that bears (the fig tree is not built; hole T7). A third of the time a young member has no adult
  keeper in its household to go to, because keepers still die young.
- Nothing was retuned after looking. Each of those would be a new arm with its own predictions.
- OPEN: why the young do worse beside a keeper; whether lineages can last in a world where everyone is mortal;
