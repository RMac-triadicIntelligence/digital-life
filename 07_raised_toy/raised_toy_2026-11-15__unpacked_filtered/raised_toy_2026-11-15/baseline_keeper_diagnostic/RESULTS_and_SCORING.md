   Keepers eat at least a tick's cost on 13% of their ticks; scouts on 37%.
2. Keepers do not start behind. In their first 20 ticks keeper founders eat 0.099 a tick and scout founders
   0.096. Newborn keepers eat 0.045 in their first 20 ticks, above cost, and hold 0.87 energy at age 20,
   up from the 0.5 they were born with. Then keepers' intake falls with age: 0.014 (ticks 20 to 80),
   0.007 (80 to 240), 0.004 (240 to 800). Scouts' falls gently and stays above cost at every age:
   0.066, 0.054, 0.048, 0.045, 0.040, 0.038.
3. Travel and calls for witness. Keepers spend 1.3% (newborn) to 4.4% (founders) of their moves going to a
   call. A newborn keeper takes 0.13 calls in its life. Calls are taken at once (mean age under one tick)
   from 8 to 11 cells away, and on arrival food above 0.3 is there 1% to 7% of the time. But the ticks spent
   travelling pay best of all: 0.075 to 0.11 a tick. Scouts never take a call (READ: the code gives them no path).
4. Harvest by kind of move. A keeper's step toward its best believed cell (82% of newborn keepers' moves)
   is followed by 0.018 intake. A scout's step toward least-known ground (75% of its moves) is followed by
   0.044. Dwelling is followed by 0.015 to 0.023 for both roles: under cost for both. Scouts take 87% of
   their intake after scout steps and 13% after dwelling.
5. Belief against the ground. At the cell a keeper chooses, it believes 0.38 to 0.41 is there. What is really
   there is 0.11 to 0.13.
6. Spacing. The spacing rule changes which cell a keeper chooses on 54% to 58% of its belief steps.
7. Birth. Keepers and scouts are born alike: food at the birth cell 0.11 and 0.12, best food in the window
   0.81 and 0.82, parent a scout 95% and 94% of the time, parent's energy 17.0 and 16.6 (median).
   2.8% of newborn keepers never eat a tick's cost (20 of 727); 1 of 370 scouts.
8. Survival. Newborn keepers: 36% alive at 10 days, 3.7% at 30 days, none at 100 days; median life 58 ticks
   (7 days). Keeper founders: median 242 ticks (30 days), longest 794. Newborn scouts: 89% alive at 30 days,
   88% at a year.
9. State at death (not a cause). Of 727 newborn keepers: 598 (82%) died moving toward a cell they believed
   held food; 128 (18%) died dwelling; 1 died on the way to a call. Keeper founders: 144 and 32.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW (kept open, as ruled)

  own remembered looks go stale as forty others eat; each look carries noise of 0.25 while the food it is
  looking for is about 0.1, and choosing the highest of many noisy memories picks the errors that ran high;
  the family map lags; spacing pushes it off its first choice more than half the time.
- Whether any one of those, changed, would keep a keeper alive. That needs a changed-rule arm. None was run.
- Why 18% die dwelling on ground that holds nothing.
- Why 45 newborn scouts die when the rest thrive.
- The first corrections note's candidates (birth energy, travel to calls, spacing) are not confirmed as causes
  by this run. Travel to calls is too rare to carry the deaths (1% to 4% of moves) and pays well when it
  happens. Birth energy does not separate the roles: both are born with 0.5 in the same places.
